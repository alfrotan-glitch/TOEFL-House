## Textbook research

- **Oxford University Press** publishes **Got it!**, a four-level American English course for secondary students. This four-book structure is **Starter, Level 1, Level 2, Level 3**, not identical to the school's five custom stages.
- Oxford's published methodology focuses on meaningful communication, contextual vocabulary/grammar, guided discovery, dialogues/pair work, the four language skills, review and self-assessment.
- Book components differ by edition/package. Oxford catalogues list combinations such as Student Book, Workbook, Teacher's Book, class audio, presentation tools and digital/online practice. The school has not confirmed whether any of these extras are included with course tuition or whether it uses First or Second Edition.
- The center's own supplementary Speaking chapter is **not** described as an official Oxford publication.
- Use of an Oxford textbook is **not** Oxford accreditation/endorsement.
